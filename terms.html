What Silverconomy is

Silverconomy, run by Prahat Consulting Services LLP, connects Silvers and their families with Care Givers (nurses, companions, physiotherapists and attendants) and lets the Silverconomy team book, follow and bill visits.

Your account

Your account is created by the Silverconomy team through an invitation. Keep your password, PIN, visit codes and exit codes private. You are responsible for what is done with your account.

Visits

A visit starts when the Silver (or their family) gives the Care Giver the visit code, and ends when they give the exit code. Silvers can cancel a visit up to 2 hours before it starts. Care Givers can ask for a change or cancellation at least 24 hours before a visit; the Silverconomy team decides.

Billing

Bills are made by the Silverconomy team and shown in the app. Only the Silverconomy team marks a bill as paid. Payment terms are agreed with the Silverconomy team.

Emergencies

Silverconomy is not an emergency service. In an emergency call your local emergency number (112 in India) first.

Ending your account

You can ask for your account to be deleted at any time (Profile, Delete my account). The Silverconomy team may disable an account that breaks these terms; you can ask for it to be reviewed from the app.
