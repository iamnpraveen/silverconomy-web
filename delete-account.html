How to delete your Silverconomy account

You can ask for your account and its data to be deleted at any time.

In the app: open Profile (your picture at the top), then Delete my account, then Ask to delete my account.

By e-mail: write to praveenkumaradha@gmail.com from the e-mail address of your account, with the subject "Delete my account".

What happens next

The Silverconomy team deletes your account within 30 days and tells you by e-mail. Deleted: your account and sign-in, profile and photo, addresses, care plan, medical record, medicines, health checks and readings, notes, messages, requests and reminders. Kept: bills and payment records only for as long as the law requires, then deleted.

If you only want to stop using the app for a while, ask the team to disable your account instead: nothing is deleted and it can be turned back on.
