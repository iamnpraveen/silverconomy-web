Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-10-30

Who we are

Silverconomy is run by Prahat Consulting Services LLP, India. It connects older people (Silvers) and their families with Care Givers (nurses, companions, physiotherapists and attendants), and lets the Silverconomy team book, follow and bill visits. Contact: praveenkumaradha@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account: name, e-mail, mobile number, profession, language, and an optional profile photo.
  • For Silvers: addresses with a Google Maps link, the care plan (daily tasks), medical record (conditions, allergies, medicines, doctor and emergency contacts), health checks and readings taken during visits, notes, service requests, visits and bills.
  • For Care Givers: category, skills, languages, area, verification status, ratings and payouts.
  • Location: only at the moment a Care Giver checks in to a visit or when someone presses the emergency (SOS) button. There is no background tracking.
  • Messages sent in the app, and messages to the AI companion.
  • Technical: app version and short error reports when something fails (no health data is included), and sign-in records.

Why we use it

To arrange and record care: booking visits, confirming arrival and departure with visit and exit codes, sharing what happened in a visit with the Silver and their family, alerting the team about late visits and readings outside a set range, billing and paying Care Givers, and keeping the service secure.

Who can see it

Only the people who need it to give care: the Silverconomy team (with the permissions the Super Admin gives them), the Care Giver assigned to a visit (from shortly before it), and family members linked to a Silver. Every time the team opens another person's account, it is recorded.

Service providers

We use Supabase (database and sign-in, hosted in Mumbai, India), Brevo (sending e-mails), Google Maps (opening the address links you add), Anthropic (replies of the AI companion, only for messages sent to the companion), and a payment provider such as Stripe when you pay a bill by card. They process data only to provide their service to us.

How long we keep it

While your account is active. When an account is deleted, its data is deleted within 30 days, except bills and payment records that the law requires us to keep, which are kept only for that period. Error reports are deleted after 90 days.

Your rights

You can see your data in the app, ask us to correct it, and ask for your account and data to be deleted: in the app (Profile, Delete my account) or by e-mail to praveenkumaradha@gmail.com. We answer within 30 days. You may also raise a grievance at the same address under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, protected by row-level access rules, and backed up nightly in encrypted form. Passwords are never stored in readable form. An app PIN and fingerprint lock are available.

Children

Silverconomy is not meant for anyone under 18. Accounts are created by the Silverconomy team.

Changes

When this policy changes, the app shows the new version and asks you to read it.
